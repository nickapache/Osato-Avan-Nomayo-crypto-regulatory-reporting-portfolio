How Osato owned the biggest crypto-regulatory story of 2024: Binance's $81bn legal war with Nigeria

In February 2024, DL News' end-of-year regulatory review confirmed it: Osato Avan-Nomayo broke the news that Nigerian officials had detained two Binance executives — Tigran Gambaryan, the US federal agent heading Binance's financial crime compliance unit, and Nadeem Anjarwalla, the company's regional chief. He then exclusively secured an interview with Nigeria's Minister of Information, Alhaji Mohammed Idris, who revealed that evidence of terrorist financing and kidnapper payments was behind the crackdown. Osato tracked every subsequent development — Anjarwalla's Ramadan escape using a hidden Kenyan passport, the Interpol red notice, Gambaryan's deteriorating health in custody, and Nigeria's extraordinary $81 billion tax penalty demand — a fine 18x larger than the $4.3bn Binance paid US authorities in 2023.
